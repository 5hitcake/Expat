ExpatBA Guide · SUBE & Phone

Sample guide — this is what the app will be full of

SUBE card & a phone line, before your DNI even arrives

Good news: neither of these waits on your DNI. Both work with just a passport, so you can knock them out in your first days.

Rules and prices change often — last checked August 2026. Treat this as a map of the process, and confirm current details with SUBE or your carrier of choice.

Phase 1 · SUBE (public transport card)

0/4

You need this for buses, subte (subway), and trains — cash isn't accepted on most of them anymore.

Phase 2 · Phone / SIM card

0/4

Start with prepago (prepaid) — postpago (postpaid) plans usually want a DNI, a CUIT/CUIL, and a credit check, which you likely won't have sorted yet.
