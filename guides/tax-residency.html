When you actually become a tax resident here

Not the 183-day rule you're probably assuming from other countries. Getting this wrong is the one mistake on this site that can genuinely cost you money.

This is the one guide here that isn't a "do it yourself" checklist. It's meant to help you ask the right questions to an actual cross-border tax advisor, not replace one — last checked August 2026. Cross-border tax mistakes are expensive and slow to fix; this genuinely isn't the place to wing it.

Phase 1 · Understand the actual trigger

0/4

Most people assume "183 days in the country" like they might have read for other places. That's not quite how Argentina does it.

Phase 2 · Check your home country's side too

0/3

Moving to Argentina doesn't automatically switch off your tax residency back home — that's a separate decision governed by your home country's own rules.

Phase 3 · Get real advice before the clock runs out

0/3

Do this well before you hit 12 continuous months, not after.
