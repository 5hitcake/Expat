Apostille & certified translations

The one step that has to happen in your home country, before you leave — miss it, and fixing it from Argentina can take months instead of weeks.

This varies by state/region and changes without much notice — last checked August 2026. Treat the country sections below as a starting map, and confirm exact current requirements with the issuing authority or your local Argentine consulate before you rely on them.

What this actually is

An apostille is a stamp/certificate that makes a public document (birth certificate, police clearance/background check, sometimes a marriage certificate) legally recognizable abroad, without a full embassy legalization process. It only works between countries that are both part of the Hague Apostille Convention — 129 countries as of 2026, including Argentina, the US, Germany, Spain, and the UK.

If your country isn't a member (e.g. the UAE, Egypt, Thailand, Malaysia, Jordan, and others), there's no apostille option — you need full consular legalization instead, which is slower and goes through your country's foreign ministry and the Argentine embassy directly.

Which documents you need depends on your visa category — check the visa guide and the residency step of the DNI/CUIT/CUIL guide for what applies to you.
