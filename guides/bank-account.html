A bank account (or the next best thing), step by step

You don't have to choose between waiting months for a "real" bank account and having no way to get paid or pay rent in the meantime. Here's what actually works at each stage.

This moves faster than government rules do — apps and fintechs change their requirements often. Treat this as a starting point, last checked August 2026, and confirm current requirements in each app before you rely on it.

Phase 1 · While your DNI is still processing

0/4

You don't need a finished DNI to start receiving money. A couple of options work with less.

Phase 2 · Once you have DNI + CUIL/CUIT

0/5

This is when every option opens up, not just the foreigner-friendly ones. Quick comparison: traditional banks (Banco Nación, Santander, BBVA, Galicia, Macro) usually mean an in-person branch appointment and sometimes proof of income; digital banks/wallets (Ualá, Brubank, Mercado Pago, Naranja X) are entirely in-app and generally faster to set up.
