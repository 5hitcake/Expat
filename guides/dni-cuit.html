DNI & CUIT, in the right order

Three separate government processes that everyone eventually has to untangle: residency (Migraciones), your ID (RENAPER), and your tax number (ARCA, formerly AFIP). Here's the order that actually works.

Rules and fees change often — last checked August 2026. Treat this as a map of the process, not a substitute for the official pages linked at the bottom, and consider a gestor or immigration lawyer if your situation isn't straightforward (marriage, employment sponsorship, prior visa issues, etc.).

Phase 1 · Residency (Migraciones)

0/5

This is the prerequisite for everything else. A Residencia Transitoria (tourist-type, short stays) does not qualify you for a DNI. You need a Residencia Temporaria (up to 3 years — Rentista, Digital Nomad, Trabajador Migrante, etc.) or Permanente.

Phase 2 · DNI (RENAPER)

0/5

Needs an approved Temporaria or Permanente residency — a precaria alone isn't enough for this step (it is enough for Phase 3, below).

Phase 3 · CUIT (ARCA, formerly AFIP)

0/5

Good news: you usually don't have to wait for your DNI to do this one — your Migraciones certificate and expediente number are often enough.
