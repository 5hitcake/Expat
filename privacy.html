Privacy

What we collect, and why

Expat BA is a small, independent project — not a company with a legal team. This page is a plain-language explanation of what happens with your data, not a formal legal document.

Newsletter

If you subscribe, your email address is stored by Mailchimp (The Rocket Science Group LLC), the service we use to send updates — usually about one email a week when new guides go up. Every email has an unsubscribe link that removes you immediately. We never sell or share your email with anyone else.

The Board

Posting doesn't require an email, password, or real name. Subscribing creates an anonymous session (a random ID with no personal info attached) that lets you post and comment — you choose whether to use a nickname or stay "Anonymous". Posts, comments, and photos you share are public and visible to anyone who visits the Board.

Photos are stored with Supabase, the backend service behind the Board. Like most web infrastructure, Supabase's servers log basic technical data (like IP addresses) at the hosting level for security and abuse prevention — this isn't something we access directly.

You can delete your own posts and comments at any time from the Board itself. Reporting a post or comment sends it to us for review; we may remove content that breaks the community guidelines.

Analytics

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics tool, to see roughly how many people visit and which pages are useful. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across sites, and doesn't collect anything that identifies you personally — just aggregate counts.

On your device

Checklist progress, your Board nickname, and a few preferences (like dismissing the install banner) are saved in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device — it's never sent to us.

Affiliate links

Some guides include links to services we've used or think are worth considering (like travel insurance or money transfer tools). A few of these are affiliate links — if you sign up through one, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only link to things we'd actually recommend, and any affiliate link is labeled as one right where it appears. This site stays free either way and always will.

Questions or requests

Want your data removed, or have a question about any of this? Reply to any Expat BA newsletter email and it'll reach us.
